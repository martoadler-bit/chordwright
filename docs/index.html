Chordvine Support

Build branching chord progressions, explore new harmonic directions, and listen to your vine unfold.

Need help or want to report a bug? Email martoadler@gmail.com.

How do I start?

Enter one chord symbol, such as Dm7 or Fmaj9, and tap Add. You can also tap a piano key to plant a major chord, generate a random progression, or open an example from Library.

How do I create a branch?

Tap any existing chord to select it, then add a chord or choose a suggestion. The new chord grows from your selection; other branches remain in the tree.

What does Play follow?

Whole tree explores the branches, returning to the root at each leaf. Selected path loops the colored route from the root to your selected chord. Pause keeps your position; Stop returns to the beginning.

How do I edit notes?

Expand Edit notes and tap piano keys to add or remove notes from the selected chord. Editing pauses playback and locks its notes. Voicing cycles through suggested note arrangements.

How do I save my work?

Your working tree is saved automatically on your device. Save path stores an independent version with its chords and settings; find it in Library. Loading stays stopped until you tap Play. Older saves that contained only references cannot restore chords that were never stored.

Can I undo a change?

Use Undo above the playback controls to recover from edits, deleted branches, or a replaced tree during your current session.

Does Chordvine support MIDI?

Open MIDI settings to enable optional output to available devices or music apps. Sound, volume, strum, chord duration, and transpose are available in Settings.

Privacy

Chordvine processes your music on your device and does not require an account. Read the full Privacy Policy for local storage, optional MIDI output, support email, and website hosting details.
