Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Chordvine works on your device. We do not collect your chord progressions, audio, or personal information through the app. No account is required.

Who we are

Chordvine is developed by Martin Adler. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Chordvine iPhone app and its support website.

Information handled by the app

Chord generation, harmonic suggestions, and audio synthesis run on your device. Chordvine does not upload your music or send it to an AI service or developer-operated server.

The app does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location.

Local storage and backups

Your working chord tree, saved paths, chord names, note edits, voicings, and musical settings are stored in the app’s storage on your device. Interface preferences, such as following playback, are also stored locally. We cannot access this information remotely.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups or transfers. Chordvine does not operate its own cloud storage or synchronization service. Apple’s handling of backups is governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Optional MIDI output

If you enable MIDI output, Chordvine sends musical messages, such as notes, velocity, and channel information, to MIDI destinations available through iOS. Those destinations can include connected hardware or other music apps. Chordvine does not send these messages to a developer-operated server.

How a receiving device, app, or MIDI service stores or forwards those messages depends on that destination and its own settings and privacy practices.

Support correspondence

If you email martoadler@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for your question.

Support correspondence may be retained while needed to handle the request and maintain relevant support records. You can request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your information or share app usage data with advertisers or data brokers. Your locally stored music is not collected by us. Support emails are processed by the email services used to deliver and answer them.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear the working tree and delete saved paths within the app. You can also disable MIDI output at any time. Deleting the app using iOS’s Delete App option removes its locally stored data from the device; offloading the app may keep that data. Backup copies, if any, are managed through Apple’s backup settings.

There is no Chordvine account to delete. To ask about support correspondence or privacy, contact martoadler@gmail.com.

Apple diagnostics

Apple may process device and app diagnostics according to your system privacy settings and its own policies. This is separate from data collection implemented in Chordvine.

This website

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when serving the website, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting a support link opens a website outside the app.

Children’s privacy

Chordvine does not collect personal information from children through the app and does not require users to provide an age or create an account.

Changes to this policy

If Chordvine’s information practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will reflect the revision.
